AI written

Ordered by how much each change moved this product's footprint, largest first. Grounded strictly in the DEFRA changes notes. Where the notes are silent, the tool says so instead of inventing a reason.

Cited Electricity generated, Electricity: UK Scope 2  ·  0.1770 to 0.1310 kg CO2e/kwh  ·  26.0% lighter 0.0391 kg 99.3% of the total change
Why it changed
A revised methodology has been introduced for calculating UK electricity emissions. The previous approach divided total electricity related emissions from the UK Greenhouse Gas Inventory by total electricity generation, which tied the factor to the inventory publication cycle and produced a two year lag.
"Revision to the calculation method for UK electricity (affected tabs: UK electricity; Transmission and distribution; indirect knock-on effects on other factors that include electricity including Rail, xEVs and Homeworking)." DEFRA 2026 full set, What's new sheet. Retrieval relevance 1.00
Methodology note
Reported under the same GHG Protocol scope classification. The change is a factor version update, so year over year comparisons should note the DEFRA release year (ISO 14064 comparability).
Target impact
This factor decreased, easing the product footprint.

Hue encodes how far a number can be trusted. Direction of travel is a glyph, a sign and a word, never a colour, so a falling footprint is never painted as an alarm: ▼ 1.68%, fell and ▲ 0.5%, rose are the same ink.

Controls

44px minimum target. Interactive edges use the ink border (19.59:1), never the decorative grey (2.08:1). Focus is the yellow block with an ink underline.

What changed, and why
Shell
A masthead that names the product and the two versions being compared, plus a sticky section nav. A long report now tells you where you are.
Setup
Three numbered steps in the canvas with a live preview of your file, instead of a sidebar that disappears below 768px.
Result
Two figures and a delta, not one string. Green moved from a slab to a rail and a pill, so the number reads as ink on paper and the completion signal stays intact.
Coverage
A meter against the 95% bar, so partial is visible rather than inferred from a percentage.
Explanations
Cards that lead with the status pill and the impact on your own footprint, with the DEFRA quote set as a source block.
Type
A system font stack, so it looks native on every platform and still loads nothing from the network. Figures are tabular everywhere.
