Explanations

Citation first. DEFRA's own sentence sits above the generated prose and is visually marked as evidence rather than commentary. The model is a reading aid, never the authority.

Electricity generated · Electricity: UK Scope 2 ✓ Cited 0.17700 → 0.13096
Official DEFRA note, quoted verbatim: "Revision to the calculation method for UK electricity. A revised methodology has been introduced: the previous approach divided total electricity-related emissions from the UK Greenhouse Gas Inventory by total generation, which introduced a two-year lag."

Why it changed. The factor fell 26.0% because DEFRA replaced the calculation method for UK grid electricity, removing a two-year reporting lag.

Methodology note. Same GHG Protocol scope classification. This is a factor-version update, so year-over-year comparisons should note the DEFRA release year (ISO 14064 comparability).
